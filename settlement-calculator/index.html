Why this is a range, not a number

Unlike statutory redundancy pay, there's no fixed formula for what a settlement agreement should be worth — it depends on your notice pay, length of service, any redundancy entitlement, accrued holiday, and a negotiated element reflecting the strength of your position. Where discrimination, whistleblowing, or another protected reason is involved, compensation is uncapped and can be substantially higher than a standard exit. This tool gives you a sensible starting point in two minutes; it is not legal or financial advice, and it cannot replace a solicitor reviewing your actual circumstances.

What typically makes up a settlement payment

  • Notice pay (or pay in lieu of notice)
  • Any redundancy payment you're entitled to — see our redundancy calculator for the statutory figure
  • Accrued but untaken holiday pay
  • A negotiated compensation element for loss of employment and agreeing not to bring claims
  • Any enhanced or contractual terms your employer offers above the legal minimum

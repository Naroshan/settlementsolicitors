Settlement Agreement Advice in Tower Hamlets

As home to Canary Wharf, Tower Hamlets sees the same settlement agreement questions we handle everywhere — is the payment fair, is it tax-free, and should you push back. For employees and employers in Tower Hamlets, a settlement agreement is a legally binding contract that ends employment on agreed terms, usually in exchange for a payment. Our team advises clients throughout Tower Hamlets and the wider East London region, making sure you understand your rights and secure the strongest possible outcome.

Why the Law Requires a Solicitor

The law deliberately makes independent advice a condition of a valid settlement agreement. Once we have advised you and signed the certificate confirming it, the agreement becomes binding — but only after you fully understand the deal and are satisfied it is right for you.

What We Check in Your Agreement

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Tower Hamlets, we look closely at:

  • The size and structure of the termination payment, and whether any of it can be paid tax-free.
  • Whether any statutory or contractual claims are being unfairly signed away.
  • The wording of the agreed reference and any announcement to colleagues.
  • Confidentiality and non-derogatory terms, and whether they are reasonable.

Who Pays for the Advice?

Almost always, yes. Employers routinely include a contribution towards the employee's legal fees, precisely because independent advice is a legal requirement. In the majority of cases in Tower Hamlets that contribution meets our costs entirely, and we will tell you up front if it does not.

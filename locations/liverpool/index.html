Settlement Agreement Advice in Liverpool

As a major port city with UNESCO maritime heritage, Liverpool sees the same settlement agreement questions we handle everywhere — is the payment fair, is it tax-free, and should you push back. Whether you have been made redundant, managed out, or offered a package to leave quietly, a settlement agreement in Liverpool should never be signed without advice. We act for people right across Merseyside, checking every clause — payment, notice, references and restrictions — before anything is finalised.

Independent Advice Is a Legal Requirement

A settlement agreement is only legally binding once you have received advice from an independent adviser, such as a qualified solicitor, on its terms and their effect on your ability to bring a claim. That safeguard exists to protect you — and it is why employers almost always contribute towards your legal fees.

What We Check in Your Agreement

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Liverpool, we look closely at:

  • The wording of the agreed reference and any announcement to colleagues.
  • The size and structure of the termination payment, and whether any of it can be paid tax-free.
  • Whether any statutory or contractual claims are being unfairly signed away.
  • The contribution your employer is making towards your legal costs.

Who Pays for the Advice?

In the vast majority of cases, your employer pays. It is standard practice for the employer to contribute towards your legal costs as part of the deal, and in most straightforward matters that contribution covers our fee in full — so there is nothing for you to pay.

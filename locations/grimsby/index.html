Settlement Agreement Advice in Grimsby

Grimsby, a historic fishing port on the Humber estuary, is in Lincolnshire, but the law around settlement agreements is the same wherever you're based. For employees and employers in Grimsby, a settlement agreement is a legally binding contract that ends employment on agreed terms, usually in exchange for a payment. Our team advises clients throughout Grimsby and the wider Lincolnshire region, making sure you understand your rights and secure the strongest possible outcome.

Why You Need Independent Legal Advice

A settlement agreement is only legally binding once you have received advice from an independent adviser, such as a qualified solicitor, on its terms and their effect on your ability to bring a claim. That safeguard exists to protect you — and it is why employers almost always contribute towards your legal fees.

Before You Sign: What We Look At

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Grimsby, we look closely at:

  • Your notice pay, holiday pay and any outstanding bonus or commission.
  • Post-termination restrictions, such as non-compete and non-solicitation clauses.
  • The contribution your employer is making towards your legal costs.
  • Confidentiality and non-derogatory terms, and whether they are reasonable.

Who Pays for the Advice?

For most people the advice costs them nothing. The settlement agreement itself will usually set out a contribution from your employer towards your legal fees, and that figure typically covers our work in full. Where a matter is more complex, we agree any difference with you before we start.

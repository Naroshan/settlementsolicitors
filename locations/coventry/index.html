Why You Need Independent Legal Advice

The law deliberately makes independent advice a condition of a valid settlement agreement. Once we have advised you and signed the certificate confirming it, the agreement becomes binding — but only after you fully understand the deal and are satisfied it is right for you.

Before You Sign: What We Look At

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Coventry, we look closely at:

  • Confidentiality and non-derogatory terms, and whether they are reasonable.
  • The wording of the agreed reference and any announcement to colleagues.
  • Post-termination restrictions, such as non-compete and non-solicitation clauses.
  • Your notice pay, holiday pay and any outstanding bonus or commission.

Will My Employer Pay My Legal Fees?

Almost always, yes. Employers routinely include a contribution towards the employee's legal fees, precisely because independent advice is a legal requirement. In the majority of cases in Coventry that contribution meets our costs entirely, and we will tell you up front if it does not.

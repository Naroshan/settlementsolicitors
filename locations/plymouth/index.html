Settlement Agreement Advice in Plymouth

As a major naval port city on the South West coast, Plymouth sees the same settlement agreement questions we handle everywhere — is the payment fair, is it tax-free, and should you push back. Whether you have been made redundant, managed out, or offered a package to leave quietly, a settlement agreement in Plymouth should never be signed without advice. We act for people right across Devon, checking every clause — payment, notice, references and restrictions — before anything is finalised.

Independent Advice Is a Legal Requirement

Because you are giving up the right to bring most employment claims, the agreement is not enforceable until an independent solicitor has advised you on what you are signing away. This requirement is in your favour: it means you get proper advice, usually at your employer's expense.

What Our Review Covers

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Plymouth, we look closely at:

  • The size and structure of the termination payment, and whether any of it can be paid tax-free.
  • Confidentiality and non-derogatory terms, and whether they are reasonable.
  • The wording of the agreed reference and any announcement to colleagues.
  • Post-termination restrictions, such as non-compete and non-solicitation clauses.

The Cost to You

For most people the advice costs them nothing. The settlement agreement itself will usually set out a contribution from your employer towards your legal fees, and that figure typically covers our work in full. Where a matter is more complex, we agree any difference with you before we start.

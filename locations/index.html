Employment law advice, wherever you are in England & Wales

Settlement agreements, redundancy and employment tribunal matters follow the same law across England and Wales, so where you live rarely changes your options — only how you prefer to meet. Every location we cover is served by the same team of SRA-regulated employment solicitors at Ackroyd Legal (London) LLP, and almost all of our advice is delivered remotely by phone, video or Microsoft Teams. That means you get the same specialist review of your agreement, your notice pay, your references and your restrictive covenants whether you are in central London or a market town on the other side of the country.

Choosing your nearest location above simply helps us tailor examples to your area and, where relevant, point you toward the correct employment tribunal region. If you cannot see your town listed, it does not matter — we advise employees and employers nationwide, and the process is exactly the same: send us your settlement agreement, we review it before your consultation, and we explain in plain English whether the terms are fair and where they can be improved. In most settlement agreement cases the employer contributes to your legal fees, so expert advice is usually more affordable than people expect.

Settlement Agreements in Manchester, Greater Manchester

Manchester is the North West's major commercial and cultural hub, in Greater Manchester. Settlement agreements are used across Greater Manchester to bring an employment relationship to a close on agreed terms — often around redundancy, a workplace dispute, or a performance issue. Wherever you are in Manchester, we review the agreement, assess the financial package, and advise you honestly on whether to sign, negotiate, or push back.

Independent Advice Is a Legal Requirement

A settlement agreement is only legally binding once you have received advice from an independent adviser, such as a qualified solicitor, on its terms and their effect on your ability to bring a claim. That safeguard exists to protect you — and it is why employers almost always contribute towards your legal fees.

What Our Review Covers

When we review a settlement agreement for a client in Manchester, we look closely at:

  • The contribution your employer is making towards your legal costs.
  • The wording of the agreed reference and any announcement to colleagues.
  • Confidentiality and non-derogatory terms, and whether they are reasonable.
  • Whether any statutory or contractual claims are being unfairly signed away.

Who Pays for the Advice?

For most people the advice costs them nothing. The settlement agreement itself will usually set out a contribution from your employer towards your legal fees, and that figure typically covers our work in full. Where a matter is more complex, we agree any difference with you before we start.
